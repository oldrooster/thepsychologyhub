Zoe provides psychological assessment and therapy for adults dealing with various mental health challenges. Her background includes work in New Zealand's correctional facilities and public health settings, which shaped her appreciation for human resilience and life complexity.

She specialises in treating anxiety, stress, depression, emotional regulation difficulties, anger, and relationship issues. Her therapeutic methodology integrates CBT as the foundation, supplemented by Motivational Interviewing and ACT principles. She emphasises helping clients identify problematic patterns, reconnect with personal values, and develop sustainable change skills through a compassionate and trustworthy therapeutic relationship.

Outside professional practice, she pursues outdoor activities including camping and ocean swimming, plus Mediterranean cuisine preparation.
