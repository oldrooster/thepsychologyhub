Margo is a registered Clinical Psychologist who offers psychological therapy and assessments for adults 16+ who are struggling with mental health difficulties.

She has professional experience in prison systems and Te Whatu Ora in Aotearoa, plus hospitals in New South Wales. Her expertise spans mood disorders, anxiety conditions, eating disorders, and adjustment issues related to physical illness. She employs an integrative approach tailored to each client's situation and supported by current research.

Her primary therapeutic methods include Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Metacognitive therapy, and mindfulness-based approaches such as Acceptance and Commitment Therapy and Dialectical Behavioural Therapy.

A therapeutic relationship based on support, respect, and evidenced-based care is important to Margo in assisting her clients to reach their goals and to live fulfilling lives.
