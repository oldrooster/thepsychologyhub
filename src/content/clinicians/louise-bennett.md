Louise brings 10 years of experience helping people of all ages with a range of nutrition needs. Her background includes extended work in mental health settings, including the South Island Eating Disorders Service and Child, Adolescent and Family (CAF) Services. She is passionate about providing personalised, evidence-based nutrition care to help people improve their health and well-being.

Her clinical specialisations encompass eating disorders (including ARFID), picky eating, ADHD, digestive health, weight management, and women's health. Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with family, mountain biking, and sports-related activities.
