With over 20 years of experience as a registered Social Worker and Therapist, I have supported young people, adults, and families across mental health, healthcare, and community settings. I have a particular interest in treating eating disorders and bring extensive experience in working with mood and anxiety disorders, trauma-related difficulties, PTSD, and major life transitions.

At the heart of my practice is a strong belief in the healing power of a safe, supportive therapeutic relationship. I strive to create a warm, non-judgmental space where you feel heard, respected, and genuinely understood. Whether you're seeking relief from psychological distress or aiming for greater balance and fulfilment, I will meet you where you are. Together, we will explore your struggles, strengths, and hopes — with the goal of supporting meaningful and lasting improvement in your mental wellbeing.

My approach is collaborative and tailored to your unique needs, drawing on evidence-based therapies such as Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) and Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR). I am trained in EMDR and particularly value its effectiveness in supporting recovery from trauma. I am also an approved ACC provider for sensitive claims.
