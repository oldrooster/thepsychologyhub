At the heart of Charis' work is the belief that healing happens in connection — with ourselves, with others, and with the emotions that shape our experiences.

Charis assists individuals, couples, and families in building stronger connections and emotional resilience. She addresses anxiety, low self-worth, trauma, grief, relationship challenges, and mood concerns by focusing on emotional and attachment-based patterns.

As a Clinical Psychologist, she integrates multiple therapeutic approaches including Emotionally Focused Therapy, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, and EMDR, combined with mindfulness and self-compassion techniques.

Outside professional work, Charis is a mother of three who enjoys creative pursuits, outdoor activities, spending time with family and friends, and caring for her extensive plant collection.

She welcomes clients across all age groups and relationship types, including children, teenagers, adults, couples, and parents.
