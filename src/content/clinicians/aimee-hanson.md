Aimee has worked as a clinical psychologist for 16 years, helping individuals and families make substantial improvements in their mental well-being. Beyond her psychology training, she holds formal qualifications in family therapy and specialises in assisting families through joint sessions.

Her clinical focus includes anxiety, trauma, dissociation, and comprehensive assessments for adult autism spectrum disorder and ADHD. She treats PTSD, phobias, OCD, worry disorders, and panic using EMDR and attachment-based family therapy approaches.

She conducts specialty mental injury assessments for ACC, including complex trauma evaluations with focus on dissociative conditions. She also provides expert reports for Family Court proceedings.

Professional affiliations include membership in the NZ College of Clinical Psychologists, EMDRNZ, and the International Society for the Study of Trauma and Dissociation.
