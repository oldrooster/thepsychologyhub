Annalise has over 20 years of experience in the mental health sector as a therapist and registered nurse with postgraduate training, working across New Zealand and UK settings.

She provides warm, collaborative, and client-centred support to clients aged 10 through older adults, specialising in adolescent and young adult mental health. She is a current Victim Support funded sessions provider.

Her background includes roles as case manager and clinician in acute/community settings, specialist DBT and ADHD teams, and adult acute mental health management.

Evidence-based therapies she offers include EMDR for trauma, DBT, Interpersonal Psychotherapy, Role Theory, and small figures work. She has particular interest in trauma, attachment styles, emotional regulation, general mental health, anxiety, depression, stress, ADHD, life transitions, and interpersonal challenges.

Outside work, she is a mother of two who enjoys outdoor exploration, travel, and yoga practice. She is currently training in Psychodrama Psychotherapy.
