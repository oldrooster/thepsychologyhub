Justine is a registered psychologist with nearly 30 years of experience working with children, adolescents, adults, and families.

She has maintained a private practice since 2004, focusing on helping clients discover strengths and maximise potential through evidence-based, collaborative approaches.

Her professional background includes roles with Child Youth & Family, school guidance counselling, residential therapy for young offenders, child and family counselling, and work with the Ministry of Education at Group Special Education.

She specialises in trauma-focused interventions, including Brainspotting training, and holds ACC Sensitive Claims provider status.

Her therapeutic approach integrates Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Acceptance & Commitment Therapy, and Narrative Therapy to suit individual client needs.
