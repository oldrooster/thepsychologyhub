Simon began working with children and youth at the Canterbury District Health Board in 2012, transitioning to private practice through Tui Psychology since 2016. He recently supported Canterbury University students at their Health Centre. His earlier career included secondary education teaching and HR work, where he managed a recruitment consultancy focused on the social sector.

His clinical experience encompasses depression, anxiety disorders (GAD, social anxiety), OCD, panic disorder, trichotillomania, emetophobia, grief, trauma, post-concussion syndrome, relationship challenges, and adjustment issues. He also assists clients with ADHD symptom management.

His therapeutic approach centres on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), which helps the individual accept those things beyond their control. He integrates compassion-focused and emotion-focused therapy concepts when appropriate.

Personally, Simon is a parent of two school-age children, enjoys tramping, surfing, mountain biking, and woodwork. He maintains long-standing involvement with Big Brothers Big Sisters Christchurch, serving as Board Chairperson from 2015–18.
