Julie is a New Zealand-trained, qualified, and registered clinical psychologist. She spent her early and mid-career treating mild-moderate mental health symptoms, primarily depression and anxiety. She then specialised in psychiatric consultation-liaison at the CDHB, developing individualised management plans for people adjusting to acute and chronic physical health diagnoses across medical specialties.

She served as Research Fellow for the University of Otago on clinical trials investigating non-cardiac chest pain, broken heart syndrome, and the psychological effects of the March 15 Christchurch terror attack on the local Muslim community, resulting in journal publications.

In 2017, Julie experienced significant personal loss with the deaths of her husband and eldest son two weeks apart. She completed a postgraduate diploma in Positive Psychology as part of her recovery. Her current clinical focus includes loss, grief, resilience, and adaptation to adversity through positive psychology strategies. She authored *Grief on the Run* (March 2021), applying the NZ Mental Health Foundation's 5 Ways to Wellbeing Model to significant hardship.

Outside work, Julie enjoys tramping, cold water swimming, her role as grandmother, and beach activities with her dog.
