Helene is a Clinical Psychologist with experience in child and adult mental health services including community mental health, medical, youth forensic, and private practice settings. She focuses on adolescents and young adults.

She conducts comprehensive mental health assessments and provides evidence-based interventions for a range of mental health difficulties such as low mood, anxiety issues, trauma, low self-esteem, obsessive-compulsive tendencies, and adjustment related issues. Her therapeutic approaches include CBT, EMDR, MCT, and ACT, delivered in a compassionate, culturally sensitive, and approachable manner. She holds ACC approval for Sensitive Claims support.

Outside work, she enjoys adventures with her little ones, catching up with family and friends, a good read, and trips away.
