Felicity has been practising as a Registered Clinical Psychologist since 2012, primarily as a Senior Psychologist at Ara Poutama, the Department of Corrections. She has experience working with individuals from diverse walks of life, and often those with complex and difficult life histories.

Her therapeutic expertise spans multiple modalities including group-based CBT, ACT, Schema Therapy, and DBT. She specialises in assessing and treating neurodivergence, trauma, anxiety, depression, self-regulation difficulties, and interpersonal challenges.

Currently, Felicity conducts assessment clinics for adult ADHD and autism. She maintains a therapeutic practice focused on previous assessment clients who would like additional time to address co-occurring mental health experiences. She describes her approach as supportive yet direct, with humour integrated throughout.

She is ACC-registered and a WINZ provider, offering pathways for client funding eligibility discussions.
