Sarah is an experienced Clinical Psychologist who has worked for more than 20 years in New Zealand and Australia in public mental health and private practice settings.

Her therapeutic focus encompasses anxiety, OCD, depression, adjustment to difficult life transitions, psychosis, and managing work, relationships, or life stress — with particular emphasis on assisting caregivers supporting individuals with health conditions.

Sarah has mentored psychologists, therapists, doctors, nurses, psychology/CBT students and other allied health practitioners in both clinical and professional contexts. She emphasises collaborative work aimed at developing a shared understanding and improving mental health and wellbeing through customised evidence-based approaches.

Personally, she appreciates cooking, travel, outdoor activities, poetry, and spending time with loved ones.
