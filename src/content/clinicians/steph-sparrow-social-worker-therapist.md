Steph holds 20 years of experience working with children, youth, families and adults in specialist mental health, health, community and residential settings. She additionally serves as a Health Improvement Practitioner in general practice.

As a qualified CBT practitioner, she addresses anxiety and mood disorders, perfectionism, age and stage transitions, gender diversity, sleep, eating disorders and trauma. Her approach integrates multiple therapeutic modalities including CBT, EMDR, ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy), and systems theory, with cultural awareness of Māori perspectives.

She provides EMDR services across all age groups and enjoys outdoor recreational activities including tramping and cycling.
