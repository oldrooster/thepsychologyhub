Anna brings over twenty years of specialist mental health experience as a registered nurse and therapist. She recently entered private practice to support clients in achieving psychological wellness.

Her therapeutic approach emphasises a non-judgmental, open, collaborative, and supportive environment tailored to each person's distinct requirements. She integrates evidence-based methods including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy, Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing, and Schema Therapy.

Her clinical expertise encompasses anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, low self-esteem, stress, trauma, neurodiversity, eating disorders, and body image concerns. Her goal involves helping clients develop greater self-compassion, identify personal strengths, strengthen resilience, and manage life's difficulties.

Outside work, she values time with family and her dog, enjoying outdoor activities like bushwalking and coastal relaxation.
