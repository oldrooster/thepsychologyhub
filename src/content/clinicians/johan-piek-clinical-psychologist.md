Johan is a clinical psychologist serving adults over 21 who face various mental health concerns and life transitions. His practice emphasises developing a deep understanding of each person's inner world and helping clients process difficult experiences.

Originally from Cape Town, he moved to New Zealand in 2019 after travelling and personal development work. He brings experience from public agency roles and now operates a full-time private practice.

He provides a therapeutic space that is calm, respectful, and non-judgmental — balancing empathy with structure to enable clients to gain insight and develop practical wellbeing strategies.

Clinical focus areas include anxiety, mood difficulties, grief, behaviour change, identity exploration, and work-related stress. He has extensive experience with diverse populations and the LGBTQIA+ community.

He specialises in neurodiversity-informed therapy, ADHD assessments, and post-diagnostic support for adults with ADHD and late-diagnosed autism. He also offers coaching for neurodiverse professionals and leaders focused on sustainable performance and strengths-based development.
