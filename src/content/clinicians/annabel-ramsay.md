Annabel brings over 16 years of experience in mental health settings, having worked in community teams and private practice. She specialises in treating adults (18+) experiencing depression, anxiety, trauma, or dealing with intense emotions, plus performance anxiety and secondary trauma for professionals and athletes.

Her therapeutic approach emphasises collaboration and building resilience through evidence-based methods. She is certified in EMDR and training as an EMDR Consultant, alongside expertise in ACT and CBT. As an ACC-approved provider for Sensitive Claims, she tailors interventions to individual needs.

Outside work, Annabel resides in Christchurch with family and actively participates in endurance and multisport competitions.
