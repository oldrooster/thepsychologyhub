Brigette has extensive experience across research, mental health, and health sectors. She is dedicated to helping people manage whatever life challenges come their way and to develop resilience.

Her clinical work encompasses depression, anxiety, eating disorders, stress, anger, trauma, grief, ADHD in adults and parents, pain management, serious injuries, cancer, and health-related concerns. She primarily employs cognitive behavioural therapy approaches.

Originally from Christchurch, Brigette has Irish Catholic heritage through her father and Scottish ancestry through her mother. She is a mother of three children. In her personal time, she pursues creative home projects — earning the family nickname "Projects."
