Psychologists and therapists in private practice vary in fees, although typically charge around $180–$250 per 50 minute session. Initial appointments may be longer with higher fees. Specialised assessments and report writing use either set rates or hourly charges.

## Psychologist / Therapist Fees

- Standard session (50 min): approximately $180–$250
- Adult ADHD assessments: $1,600–$1,800 (GST inclusive)
- Adult ASD assessments: $1,000–$1,600 (GST inclusive)

Please contact your chosen clinician directly to confirm their current fees.

## Dietitian Fees

- Initial assessment (60–90 min): $230
- Follow-up session (60 min): $200
- Follow-up session (30 min): $115

## Funding Options

**ACC** — ACC funds therapy for sexual abuse trauma through Integrated Services for Sensitive Claims. Some clinicians are also approved for ACC mental injury assessments.

**Health Insurance** — Clinical psychology may be covered by some private health insurance plans. Check with your provider.

**WINZ** — Work and Income New Zealand may provide partial funding for therapy for eligible clients.

**Transgender Wellbeing** — Some clinicians offer Transgender Wellbeing services via CDHB with a GP referral.

**March 15 Mosque Shooting** — Survivors may qualify for ACC or Victim Support funding. Please speak with your clinician.
