*Ehara taku toa i te toa takitahi, engari he toa takitini — Success is not the work of an individual, but the work of many.*

The Psychology Hub is a shared workspace in Riccarton, Christchurch where independent registered mental health professionals operate their own separate private practices. The Hub is directed by Anna, Brigette, Aimee, and Jo, who manage the premises.

## How We Work

Each clinician at The Psychology Hub runs their own independent business. They manage their own referrals, appointments, fees, and clinical records. The Psychology Hub Ltd holds no clinical responsibility for clients engaging with individual practitioners.

- Individual clinicians maintain independent practices
- Referrals are made directly to clinicians, not to The Psychology Hub
- Clinical records remain confidential and separate to each practitioner
- The Psychology Hub is not an employer of the clinicians working here

## Complaints

If you have a concern about the care you have received, please raise it directly with your clinician in the first instance. If this is not possible, complaints can be directed to the relevant registration body or to the Health and Disability Commissioner (HDC).
