The Psychology Hub offers comprehensive psychological assessments for adults, including assessments for ADHD and autism spectrum disorder (ASD).

  <div class="two-col__text">
    <h2>Adult ADHD Assessments</h2>
    <p>ADHD assessments are comprehensive and time intensive. They involve a clinical interview (3–4 hours), completion of psychometrics (questionnaires), gathering information from a parent/caregiver or partner, and reviewing school reports.</p>
    <p><strong>Fee:</strong> $1,600–$1,800 (GST inclusive)</p>
    <p><strong>Requirements:</strong> GP referral required. School reports must be provided. Contact details for a parent/caregiver or partner who can provide information about your childhood. Assessments are not available for individuals with concurrent substance abuse issues.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="two-col two-col--img-right">
  <div class="two-col__text">
    <h2>Adult ASD Assessments</h2>
    <p>ASD assessments typically take 2–3 hours of face-to-face assessment and require childhood developmental information from a reliable source — ideally a parent. Without this, a definitive diagnosis is unlikely.</p>
    <p><strong>Fee:</strong> $1,000–$1,600 (GST inclusive)</p>
    <p><strong>Please note:</strong> These assessments do not assess individuals with learning difficulties.</p>
