    <h2>Therapy</h2>
    <p>Good therapy is a place where you can not only reduce distressing psychological symptoms, but also learn about yourself, improve your relationships with others and build resilience.</p>
    <p>The clinicians at The Psychology Hub are registered health professionals providing evidence-based treatment. Therapeutic modalities available include CBT, MCT, Schema Therapy, ACT, DBT, Systemic Family Therapy, FBT (Family Based Treatment), Interpersonal Psychotherapy, and EMDR.</p>

    <h2>Eating Disorder Treatment</h2>
    <p>Evidence-based treatments for eating disorders are available, including Family Based Treatment and CBT-based approaches. GP involvement is typically required. Dietitian consultation is recommended where appropriate.</p>
    <p>The South Island Eating Disorders Service offers publicly funded treatment, accessible via GP referral.</p>

    <h2>EMDR</h2>
    <p>Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing (EMDR) is an extensively researched, effective psychotherapy method proven to help people recover from trauma and conditions including PTSD, anxiety, depression, and panic disorders.</p>
    <p>EMDR therapy does not require talking in detail about the distressing issue or completing homework between sessions. Many of our clinicians offer EMDR as a standalone or integrated treatment; some provide group EMDR for specific presentations. Several are ACC approved providers for Sensitive Claims.</p>
